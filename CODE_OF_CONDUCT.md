# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and evidence-led. Welcome newcomers and differing scientific viewpoints. Critique methods rather than people; document uncertainty and credit contributions. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions or restrict participation, explaining the decision when safe. Report concerns through the maintainer contact on the linked portfolio. Avoid sharing sensitive details publicly. This lightweight policy applies to repository discussions, issues and pull requests.
